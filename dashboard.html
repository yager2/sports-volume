Comparable across all seven. Contracts = contracts traded, one-sided (a buy matched with a sell is one), each a $1-max-payout instrument — this is the metric every one of these venues reports as its own "volume," and what DeFi Rate / DefiLlama / Pew publish. Notional $ re-weights each contract by its volume-weighted traded price — roughly the dollars that actually changed hands (~⅓ of the contract figure). Same definition for every venue in both columns.

Two things the metric can't normalise out. Market-making: Kalshi runs aggressive maker-incentive programs (the CFTC warned it about them in Aug 2026), so a larger share of its reported volume is automated liquidity than at the others — a real difference between the exchanges, not a counting artifact. Reporting lag: Kalshi's daily total (DeFi Rate) trails ~2 days; DKeX / UDX / Rothera finalise overnight, so a hard-outlier-low latest day is marked provisional.

Coverage. Kalshi is its whole exchange (every category); Polymarket US is prefix-filtered to sports; Novig / DKeX / UDX are 100% sports. Crypto.com is the Nadex / CDNA "EVENTS" book — Crypto.com's own OG prediction market plus FanDuel Predicts, whose entire sports flow clears there since mid-2026 — less a ~0.2% non-sports tail; its $-price has no per-market feed so its Notional $ uses the day's cross-venue average price. Rothera is the CFTC exchange behind Robinhood's prediction market (Part-16 daily report), prefix-filtered to sports — MLB-heavy — dropping a small US-macro / elections tail. Combo % is parlay volume over that venue's sports book. Novig / DKeX / Polymarket US / UDX / Crypto.com come from each one's combo product line. Kalshi is its multi-event parlay engine (the `KXMVE` series — a leg-check found these ~100% sports legs despite the "cross-category" name), measured from a ~3M-trade sample of the public feed and scaled to the daily total, so its figure moves less day to day than the report-based ones. Kalshi's % is of its whole-exchange volume (matching its main column) — ~48% by contracts, ~11% by dollars; parlay legs are many and cheap. The table's Combo column blends the five report-based venues on a common base; Kalshi's sits on its card and the Parlay-share tab. Rothera runs no on-exchange parlay.
