Kalshi Real Volume-weighted average price from Kalshi's own trade feed — the sports figure prices each series off its exact per-trade VWAP; the whole-exchange total uses a ~5M-trade sample of the public feed, refreshed weekly. Separately: the whole-exchange contract count itself (not this price) comes from DeFi Rate, who display it abbreviated (e.g. "$2.09B") — about ±0.3% built-in rounding versus the exact per-market figures the other six venues give us.
Polymarket US Proxy Bid/offer midpoint per market. The daily report has no realized trade-price column, so this is the closest published quote to a fill price — a real number from Polymarket US, just not the fill itself.
Novig Real Each market's actual closing trade price (falls back to the day's open, then the high/low midpoint if neither is populated).
DKeX Proxy Each market's daily High/Low midpoint — DraftKings' report doesn't publish a single settlement or last-trade price.
UDX Estimated UDX publishes no price data at all. This figure is entirely borrowed: that day's contract-weighted average price across the venues that do publish one, applied to UDX's own (real) contract count. Treat it as a rough placeholder, not UDX's own number.
Crypto.com Estimated The Nadex/CDNA bulletin carries no price column either. Same cross-venue-average substitute as UDX — not Crypto.com's own data.
Rothera Proxy Each market's daily high/low midpoint (falls back to close, then open).

Contract counts (Taker Volume) are exact for all seven venues — a straight sum of each one's own reported trades, no estimation anywhere. Only the Notional $ column varies in reliability: Real = the venue's own trade or close price · Proxy = a real quote from that venue, but a midpoint stand-in for the actual fill · Estimated = that venue publishes no price at all, so the figure is borrowed from the other five.

Comparable across all seven. Taker Volume = contracts traded, one-sided (a buy matched with a sell is one fill, not two — the taker's side, not maker + taker), each a $1-max-payout instrument — this is the metric every one of these venues reports as its own "volume" (Kalshi's own API calls it "taker volume" outright), and what DeFi Rate / DefiLlama / Pew publish. Notional $ re-weights each contract by its volume-weighted traded price — roughly the dollars that actually changed hands (~⅓ of the taker-volume figure). Same definition for every venue in both columns; re-verified against each venue's live source on 2026-09-21 (see VOLUME_METHODOLOGY.md). Not every venue's Notional $ rests on the same footing, though — see the "Where the Notional $ figure comes from" table below for which are real prices and which are estimated.

Two things the metric can't normalise out. Market-making: Kalshi runs aggressive maker-incentive programs (the CFTC warned it about them in Aug 2026), so a larger share of its reported volume is automated liquidity than at the others — a real difference between the exchanges, not a counting artifact. Reporting lag: Kalshi's daily total (DeFi Rate) trails ~2 days; DKeX / UDX / Rothera finalise overnight, so a hard-outlier-low latest day is marked provisional.

Coverage. This page shows each venue's sports volume (single-leg + parlays). Novig / DKeX / UDX are 100% sports; Polymarket US, Crypto.com and Rothera are the sports slice of a wider book; Kalshi is its single-leg sports plus its sports parlay engine. Whole-exchange totals for every venue are in the accompanying spreadsheet's "Total exchange" tab, and Kalshi's category breakdown is in the panel below. Crypto.com = the Nadex / CDNA "EVENTS" book (Crypto.com OG plus FanDuel Predicts) less a ~0.2% non-sports tail; Rothera = the CFTC exchange behind Robinhood, MLB-heavy, minus a small US-macro / elections tail. Both estimate Notional $ with the day's cross-venue average price (no per-market price feed). Combo % is parlay volume over that venue's sports book — Novig / DKeX / Polymarket US / UDX / Crypto.com from each combo product line; Kalshi from its `KXMVE` multi-event engine (leg-checked ~100% sports legs), a ~5M-trade sample (refreshed weekly) scaled to the daily total — ~63% by contracts but ~18% by dollars of Kalshi's whole exchange (parlay legs are many and cheap; this sample-based split can move noticeably week to week, see VOLUME_METHODOLOGY.md). The table's Combo column blends the five report-based venues; Kalshi's sample-based figure sits on its card. Rothera runs no on-exchange parlay.
