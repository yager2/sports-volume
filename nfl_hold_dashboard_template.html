Taker hold tracker

updated

Sportsbooks only reveal their hold (the share of bets they keep) when states publish monthly reports. This page estimates it early from Kalshi: the traders who cross the spread (takers) behave like sportsbook customers, so their loss rate is a stand-in for book hold. Positive = takers lost (good for the house), shown green. Negative = takers won, shown red. It is directional, not exact.

How to read this
  • Taker = the side of a Kalshi trade that crossed the spread (hit an existing order). Treated as the “customer”.
  • Hold incl. fees = −(taker profit − taker fees) ÷ taker dollars. It’s the closest match to a sportsbook’s hold, since the book keeps both the customer’s losses and its vig.
  • Expected hold uses closing prices (the final 30 minutes before kickoff) instead of results, so it only exists for pre-game bets.
  • Voided markets (inactive player, postponed game) and negotiated block trades are left out.
  • Kalshi’s NFL mix (lots of in-game trading, few parlays here) differs from a sportsbook’s, so read direction and size of swings, not exact levels. Parlays are tracked separately later.
